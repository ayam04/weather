## MET Norway Locationforecast 2.0

Source: https://api.met.no/weatherapi/locationforecast/2.0/
What we use it for: all live forecasts (current conditions, hourly, daily, tiles).
Commercial use allowed: yes. "All data and products are licensed under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD) 2.0 and Creative Commons 4.0 BY International licences" (https://api.met.no/doc/License). CC BY 4.0 permits commercial use.
Credit required: "Credit should be given to The Norwegian Meteorological Institute, shortened MET Norway". Shown in the footer of every page and under the detail tiles ("Forecast data from MET Norway"). We must indicate changes (done: "reformatted by us"). Must not suggest MET endorses us (data sources page says so). Must not use the name "Yr" or its logo.
Call or usage limits: identifying User-Agent required; truncate coordinates to 4 decimals; cache and respect Expires; over 20 requests per second total needs an agreement; tell users when data was retrieved (done: "Updated HH:MM local time"). https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService
Date checked: 4 November 2026. Re-check by: 4 February 2027.

## NOAA GHCN-Daily

Source: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/ (daily-summaries via the NCEI Access Data Service)
What we use it for: monthly climate averages on the 25 destination pages, 2015 to 2024.
Commercial use allowed: yes. US government work, public domain in the United States.
Credit required: none legally; we credit NOAA GHCN-Daily and name the station on each page.
Date checked: 4 November 2026. Re-check by: 4 February 2027.

## NASA POWER

Source: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
What we use it for: fallback climate averages where no station qualified (see REVIEW.md), and a rainfall cross-check.
Commercial use allowed: no restriction found in the POWER documentation; NASA asks for a reference ("data was obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project"). Not independently confirmed for advertising use; re-check before ads go live.
Credit required: reference requested. Named on each page that uses it and on the data sources page.
Date checked: 4 November 2026. Re-check by: 4 February 2027.
